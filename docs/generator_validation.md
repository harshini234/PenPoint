## Sanity checks

- 45-day BRD mortality (mean of lots): 0.71%. Whole-feeding-period, all-cause steer death loss in Kansas was 1.74% in 2017 (Front Vet Sci 2023), so a 45-day BRD-only figure should sit below it.
- High-risk tail: light (<550 lb), unpreconditioned sale-barn lots would average 24.7% morbidity without metaphylaxis; research herds of high-risk calves report 48% (Wottlin 2021) and 51.5% (Pillen 2016), so our extreme tail is likely conservative.
- Lots given metaphylaxis under current practice: 48.7% (context only: NAHMS 2017 p4 reports the share of *feedlots*, not lots - 39.3% of large feedlots group-treat some arriving cattle - so the two are not directly comparable).
